What we do

Care that meets people where they are

Every service below is delivered by qualified volunteers and partner clinics — and every one of them is free.

🩺

Free health checks

Blood pressure, glucose, BMI and general screenings at pop-up clinics in local neighbourhoods.

💬

Talk to a professional

Confidential advice from nurses and counsellors, in person or over a secure call.

🚑

Emergency support

Fast referrals and transport to partner hospitals when something can't wait.

🌱

Wellness & prevention

Nutrition workshops, vaccination drives and mental-health first-aid training.

👩‍👧

Family & maternal care

Prenatal check-ups, postnatal follow-ups and child growth monitoring.

🤝

Community outreach

Home visits for older people and anyone who cannot travel to a clinic.

Is there a charge? No. Never.

Health is a non-profit community programme. Every consultation, screening and referral on this page is funded by donations and delivered at zero cost to you. There are no hidden fees and no sign-up charges — if anyone asks you for money on our behalf, it isn't us.
