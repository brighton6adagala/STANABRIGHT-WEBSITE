About Mfangano Central SDA Church

A community of faith anchored in God's love, serving the people of Mfangano Island and beyond with hope, healing, and wholeness.

Who We Are

Our Church Family

Rooted in Faith, Reaching Out in Love

Mfangano Central SDA Church is a vibrant Seventh-day Adventist congregation located on the beautiful Mfangano Island in Lake Victoria, Kenya. We are a family of believers committed to following Jesus Christ, sharing His love, and preparing for His soon return.

Our church has been a beacon of light in the community for nearly a century, offering spiritual guidance, education, health services, and community development programs that transform lives and uplift families.

"We are called to be the hands and feet of Jesus — serving our community with compassion, integrity, and the unwavering hope of the gospel."

Whether you're a long-time member or a first-time visitor, you'll find a warm welcome at Mfangano Central. We worship together, grow together, and serve together as one body in Christ.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🌍

Our Mission

To proclaim the everlasting gospel of Jesus Christ to the people of Mfangano Island and the surrounding regions, making disciples, nurturing believers, and preparing a people for the soon-coming of our Lord.

🌟

Our Vision

To be a lighthouse of hope and healing — a church where every person experiences God's love, grows in faith, and is equipped to serve their community with excellence and compassion.

Our Journey

A Rich History of Faith

1924

The first Seventh-day Adventist missionaries arrive on Mfangano Island, planting the seeds of the gospel.

1928

The Mfangano Central SDA Church is officially established, with a small congregation meeting in a modest structure.

1950

A permanent church building is constructed, becoming a spiritual landmark on the island.

1975

The church expands its outreach, establishing schools and health clinics to serve the community.

2000

Major renovations and expansion of the church facilities to accommodate a growing congregation.

2024

Mfangano Central SDA Church celebrates 100 years of God's faithfulness, with a renewed vision for the future.
