Rooted in Faith, Reaching Out in Love

Mfangano Central SDA Church is more than a building — it's a family transformed by the love of Jesus. Located on beautiful Mfangano Island, Lake Victoria, we've been a beacon of hope for nearly a century.

"Come as you are — you are loved, you belong, and you matter to God and to us."

Whether you're a long-time member or visiting for the first time, you'll find a warm welcome, authentic community, and a place to grow spiritually.
